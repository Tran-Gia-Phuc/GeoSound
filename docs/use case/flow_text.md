## Video 4. izi.TRAVEL: CMS overview

▶️ **Link:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9roMOGrgR9Q&list=PLQGEGPXj0eBBQTKBOJ-2XUVH2pCnoCSrn&index=4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9roMOGrgR9Q&list=PLQGEGPXj0eBBQTKBOJ-2XUVH2pCnoCSrn&index=4)

### Script

First of all you register at izi.TRAVEL After you log in, you can create your own museum. Position it on the map and fill in general information about it Then you can start adding items to your museum collection To the each item you assign a description, audio files, pictures, even videos. While you are working in the collection, artefacts are not visible to the public at all. To make them visible you have to create an “exhibition” and place items into it. You can feel the “exhibition” with artefacts simply by “draging and droping” them there. Also you can call an “exhibition” anything you want. Even without using the word “exhibition” at all.

While testing you can still hide items from the public by keeping the exhibition in “Limited Access Mode”. Only those who enter the passcode in mobile application settings will be able to see the exhibition while it’s in Limited Access mode. When you’re happy you’ve tested it fully – you turn on Public Access Mode and let your visitors enjoy your audio guide. For more information see our tutorials at the web side.
